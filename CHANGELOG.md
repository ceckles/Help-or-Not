# Help or Not

## 1.2.1
- Marked only keeps friendly nameplates visible for players who already had them on. Marked players still get their stamps.
- New broker plugin: add "Help or Not" to an EllesmereUI data bar (Broker Plugin block), Titan Panel or similar. Shows marked players in your group; left-click opens your list, right-click opens Recent.

## 1.2.0
- Recent players: the last 50 people you grouped with, with where and when, so you can mark them after they leave. `/hon recent` or the Recent button.
- KOS kills: counts killing blows on KOS players by you or your group and shows the tally in their tooltip. Needs the combat log, so it may not count on clients that hide it from addons.

## 1.1.0
- New KOS (kill on sight) category for your PvP nemesis list: purple skull KILL ON SIGHT stamp, a raid warning when their nameplate shows up near you, and last seen time and zone in their tooltip.
- New Options view in /hon (or /hon options, or Esc > Options > AddOns > Help or Not).
- Turn each feature on or off: Helpful stamps, pulsing stamps, KOS sightings, chat, whisper, trade, invite and duel blocking, and the right-click menu.

## 1.0.1
- Fixed a freeze when marking, removing, or changing a player on Retail.
- Unit frame stamp now fits the health and mana bars on Blizzard frames.
- Nameplate stamp now sits on the player's name instead of floating above it.
- Added /hon perf and /hon scan troubleshooting commands.

- First release.
